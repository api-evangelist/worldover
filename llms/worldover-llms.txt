# Worldover

> Worldover™ is the AI operating system for chemicals and cosmetics companies. It replaces ERP, PLM, QMS, LIMS and regulatory tools with one AI-orchestrated system, custom-built around the way each team works. Powered by Willow, our chemical and regulatory intelligence engine. Live in three months. At least 30% cheaper than your current software stack, guaranteed. Backed by Index Ventures, Chalfen and EF. HQ in Camden, London.

## What Worldover does
- Replaces fragmented ERP, PLM, QMS, LIMS, regulatory, R&D, quality and FP&A tools with one system, customised to each business.
- Provides AI agents for demand planning, regulatory monitoring, quality, formulation R&D, project management, supply chain, SDS authoring and PLM updates, all running inside live customer data with permissions, approvals and audit trails.
- Powers every workflow with a chemical intelligence engine called Willow, which understands ingredient identity, jurisdictional regulatory limits, substitution implications and the relationships between substances.

## Who Worldover serves
- Beauty brands (indie to global)
- Manufacturers
- Ingredient providers and distributors
- Pharma and supplements companies
- Chemicals and cosmetics companies that need software shaped around their real workflows

## Key facts
- Headquartered in London, United Kingdom.
